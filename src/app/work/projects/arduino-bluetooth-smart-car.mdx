## Overview

A four-wheel smart car built on an Arduino Uno and controlled by smartphone commands over Bluetooth.

## Hardware

- Arduino Uno
- HW-130 motor shield
- Four DC motors
- HC-05 Bluetooth module

## Software

- AFMotor library for motor control
- Serial command handling for smartphone control over Bluetooth

## What I worked through

- Wiring and driving four DC motors through the motor shield
- Pairing and command handling over HC-05 Bluetooth
- Troubleshooting power delivery to keep the motors and controller stable
