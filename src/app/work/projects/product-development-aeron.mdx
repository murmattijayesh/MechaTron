## Overview

At Aeron Systems I work on mechanical design and product development for engineering products used in industrial, IoT and renewable-energy monitoring applications. My work takes designs from requirements through to production-ready engineering documentation.

## Product families

- **XTM-930**: CAD assemblies, drawings, documentation and engineering workflow activities.
- **XTM-920**: product development and mechanical design support across assemblies, drawings and documentation.
- **DLG88 Series**: product development, documentation and design activities.
- **PT1000**: mechanical design and engineering support for sensor-related product development.
- **RIGEL2-MT-RTD1K**: sensor packaging, assemblies, drawings and documentation.

## What I do

- Design components, assemblies and enclosures in SolidWorks
- Produce detailed engineering drawings, GA drawings and manufacturing documentation
- Create BOMs and engineering documentation
- Make design modifications and product improvements
- Apply DFM/DFA, GD&T and engineering drawing practice
- Support prototype development and validation
- Coordinate across electronics, manufacturing and engineering teams
- Run design reviews and drawing quality checks
- Handle engineering change and product release

## Typical workflow

Requirement → Concept / Design → 3D CAD → Assembly → Drawing → DFM/DFA → Prototype / Validation → BOM & Documentation → Engineering Release

Alongside product work, I improve the engineering workflow itself with SolidWorks automation and review utilities. See [GA Drawing Automation](/work/ga-drawing-automation) and [Mech AI Suite](/work/mech-ai-suite).
