## Overview

A set of home automation and IoT builds exploring how sensors, controllers and connected devices can automate everyday tasks.

## Platforms

- Arduino Uno
- Raspberry Pi Zero 2 W
- Wi-Fi IoT devices with Google Home integration

## Sensors and actuators

- HC-SR04 ultrasonic, PIR, DHT11, LDR and IR sensors
- Relays, servo motors, DC motors and motor drivers
- LCD displays
- HC-05 / HC-06 Bluetooth modules

## What it taught me

The same thinking behind my CAD automation: find something repetitive, give it structured inputs, and let the system handle it.
