## The question

Can engineering drawings and design workflows be reviewed faster and more systematically with software?

Mech AI Suite is my answer in progress: a toolkit that brings automated checks, review logic and productivity tools into the designer's existing CAD workflow.

## Why I built it

Traditional CAD workflows depend heavily on manual checking. A designer repeatedly inspects:

- Dimensions and drawing information
- Missing details
- Drawing standards
- Model and drawing consistency
- Documentation and design requirements

The goal is to move these checks from **manual inspection → software-assisted review**.

## SolidWorks integration

The suite includes custom, in-built **SolidWorks add-ins** and engineering utilities that run alongside the designer's CAD environment. Checks and productivity features live where the work actually happens, so there's no separate tool to switch to.

## What it assists with

- Drawing review and quality improvement
- Design and engineering consistency checks
- Documentation review
- Repetitive design tasks
- Project-level engineering workflows

## Long-term vision

Mech AI Suite reflects where I'm heading: **Mechanical Engineering + CAD APIs + Automation + AI**. The aim is intelligent engineering tools rather than isolated CAD command automation.

> From CAD automation to engineering intelligence.
